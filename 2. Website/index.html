MSR Honey Nut Mix for PCOS Natural Support

MSR Honey Nut Mix is specially beneficial for women managing Polycystic Ovary Syndrome (PCOS) naturally. The combination of pumpkin seeds, sunflower seeds, and sesame seeds in our mix directly supports the seed cycling protocol — a natural dietary approach widely recommended by nutritionists for hormonal balance in PCOS.

Seed cycling involves eating pumpkin and flax seeds during the first half of the menstrual cycle (follicular phase) and sunflower and sesame seeds during the second half (luteal phase). MSR Honey Nut Mix contains all four types of seeds along with Omega-3-rich walnuts that help reduce inflammation and regulate hormones naturally.

Raw honey reduces systemic inflammation, which is a key factor in PCOS. Almonds and cashews provide zinc and magnesium which support ovarian function. Women in Pakistan searching for natural PCOS treatment, PCOS seed cycling products, or hormonal balance foods will benefit from MSR Honey Nut Mix.
